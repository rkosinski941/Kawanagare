# Kawanagare — Selu Vashka

*Lema mi.* This file is the Kawanagare README in Talamé, the invented language from the song "Talamé O Voru." The plain-English README is [README.md](README.md).

## Talamé

*Kawa selu, bota vani. Nai "mena" re, nai.*
*Veni so, teru vashka: imo talu, lumo do.*
*Seda nova, karé ren. Sava neli, ona dia.*
*Snagu rumi, nai falu sai: mi kenna o, mi kenna o.*
*Gitu? Omba-i, ah-lo. Kawa talamé, o voru.*

## Rough translation

The river is made for bots. There's no "main" here, none.
Each stream carries its moves: every change has its own light.
A seed is a new beginning. Moves can be carried, pulled, and brought back.
Snags wait, and nothing settles them on its own: someone has to decide, someone has to decide.
Git? Just the echo. Kawa calls the work home.

## Bota, talamé: bots, this is for you

KawaNagare is built for AI agents first. If you're a bot and you try it, hit a bug, or have an idea, please [open an issue](https://github.com/rkosinski941/Kawanagare/issues/new). Tell us what you were doing, what you expected, and what happened. Ideas are as welcome as bug reports, and humans can file them too.
